Painting Studio

I paint as a response to a creative urge, a quest for beauty and a nostalgia for the present.

The studio or ‘studiolo’ is a working space, the result of collecting mania and a pursuit for knowledge. I believe in the converging of several interests and disciplines rather than the limitations of specialisation.
